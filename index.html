Our Purveyors & Philosophy

Rooted in European Tradition, Celebrating Local Artisans

At The Copper Crock Deli, we scour pastoral dairy valleys, historic aging cellars, and small independent smokehouses to bring you provisions of uncompromising integrity. Every wedge of cheese and batch of butter reflects generations of master craftsmanship.

Our relishes and preserves adhere strictly to a zero-water principle. We simmer seasonal orchard fruits in their natural juices without water or artificial additives, achieving unmatched depth and complexity for your charcuterie boards.
